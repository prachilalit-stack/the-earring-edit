A LITTLE TRANSPARENCY

Your answers, your choice.

This survey helps plan a potential student earring collection. We ask about style, spending preferences, and your experience wearing earrings. No name or contact information is requested.

Submitted answers are saved for the project owner to review. The rant corner is optional. Rants stay with the project owner unless you tick “Share my rant anonymously on this wall”. Shared rants are visible to everyone taking the survey; your other answers stay private. Please leave out names, contact details, and other people’s private information. No purchase is required.

The illustrations are style references, not products available to order.
