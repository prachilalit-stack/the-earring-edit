Your answers, your choice.

This survey helps plan a potential student earring collection. We ask about style, past purchases, and your experience wearing earrings. No contact information is requested in the survey. After submitting, a separate optional email signup lets you request sample or launch updates; it is not linked to your survey answers.

Submitted answers are saved for the project owner to review. The rant corner is optional. Rants stay with the project owner unless you tick “Share my rant anonymously on this wall”. Shared rants are shown after submission; your other answers stay private. Please leave out names, contact details, and other people’s private information. No purchase is required.

The illustrations are style references, not products available to order.
